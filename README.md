# Deezer SEO & GEO Optimization Strategy

### Project Overview
A proposed SEO and Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) strategy designed to improve Deezer's organic search visibility, AI search discoverability, and user engagement.

### Key Focus Areas
- Technical SEO auditing
- Keyword research and mapping
- On-page SEO optimization
- Content strategy for Deezer Flow and playlist transfer
- Backlink outreach to 30 relevant music and technology publications
- AI search visibility and content optimization
- KPI tracking and performance measurement

### Tools & Methodology
Google Search Console, GA4, SEMrush, Moz, Screaming Frog, Excel and Power BI.

### Expected Outcomes
- Improved non-branded search visibility
- Stronger internal linking and content discoverability
- Increased organic traffic and user registrations
- Improved AI search presence
- Better measurement of SEO performance

### Project Status
Independent portfolio proposal. Recommendations and targets are illustrative and have not been implemented or validated by Deezer.

### Deliverables
SEO & GEO strategy workbook containing proposed audits, recommendations, keyword mapping and backlink opportunities.
